# Renewal - Value Recap & Results Review

---

**Subiect**: [Subiect value: "Your [product] results: [timeframe]" sau "[Rezultat impressive] în [period]" sau "Renewal coming: here's your ROI"]

---

Salut [Prenume],

Renewal-ul tău e în [timeframe] - dar înainte de asta, vreau să îți arăt ce ai realizat.

## În ultimele [period - ex: "12 luni"], cu [produs]:

📊 [Metric #1]: [Their specific result]
📊 [Metric #2]: [Their specific result]
📊 [Metric #3]: [Their specific result]

**ROI estimate**: [Calculație value vs. cost - ex: "ai economisit ~[amount]" sau "return de [X]x"]

## Ce se întâmplă cu renewal:

✅ Păstrezi toate [features/beneficii actuale]
✅ Access la [new features launched since they joined]
✅ [Loyalty bonus/benefit pentru renewal]

**Preț**: [Renewal price] ([compare to value/ROI])

**Auto-renewal**: [Data] - nu trebuie să faci nimic.

**Preferi să discutăm?** [Link calendar pentru renewal review call]

Mulțumesc că ești parte din [community/customer base]!

[Numele tău]

P.S. - [Appreciation - ex: "Feedback-ul tău a ajutat la [specific improvement]. Keep it coming!"]

---

**Placeholder-uri pentru AI**:
- `[Prenume]`: First name (ton: valued relationship)
- `[timeframe]`: When renewal is (ton: heads up timing)
- `[period]`: How long they've been customer (ton: relationship duration)
- `[produs]`: Product name (ton: context)
- `[Metric #1-3]`: THEIR actual usage/results (ton: personalized proof of value)
- `[Their specific result]`: Individual outcomes (ton: data-driven celebration)
- `[Calculație value]`: ROI calculation (ton: justify renewal)
- `[amount]`: Savings/value generated (ton: quantified benefit)
- `[X]x`: Return multiple (ton: investment perspective)
- `[features/beneficii]`: What they keep (ton: continuity)
- `[new features]`: What's improved (ton: getting more value)
- `[Loyalty bonus]`: Renewal perk (ton: reward loyalty)
- `[Renewal price]`: Cost (ton: transparent)
- `[compare to value]`: ROI framing (ton: value perspective)
- `[Data]`: Renewal date (ton: specific timing)
- `[Link calendar]`: Review call option (ton: personal touch)
- `[community/customer base]`: Belonging (ton: appreciation)
- `[Numele tău]`: Your name (ton: relationship owner)
- `[specific improvement]`: How they influenced product (ton: valued input)

**Note de personalizare**:
- Show THEIR actual data (not generic claims)
- ROI calculation = justify continued investment
- Highlight new features since they joined = ongoing value
- Auto-renewal note = no action required (smooth)
- Offer review call = personal touch for high-value customers
- Appreciation = strengthen relationship
- Works 30 days before renewal (time to decide)
- Can segment: auto-send for low-touch, personal call for enterprise
- Track: renewal rate improvement with value recap
- Follow with: thank you for renewal, or retention call if churning
