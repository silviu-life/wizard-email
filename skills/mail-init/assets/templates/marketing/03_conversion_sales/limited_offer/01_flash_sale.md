# Limited Offer - Flash Sale

---

**Subiect**: [Subiect urgent: "[Hours/Days] left: [Discount %]" sau "Flash: [Beneficiu] at [Price]" sau "Last chance: [Oferă]"]

---

Salut [Nume],

**[Timeframe exact - ex: "24 ore"] rămas.**

[Nume produs/serviciu] la [Discount % sau pricing special - ex: "40% off" sau "$X în loc de $Y"].

## De ce acest sale?

[Motiv real pentru discount - ex: "Sărbătorim [milestone]" sau "Black Friday special" sau "End of quarter clearance"]

## Ce primești:

• [Produs/serviciu principal - quick description]
• [Feature/bonus #1]
• [Feature/bonus #2]
• **BONUS:** [Time-limited bonus doar pentru flash sale]

**Regular price:** [Preț normal - strikethrough visual]
**Flash price:** [Preț discount] - **Save [Amount/Percentage]**

## Cine a cumpărat deja:

"[Testimonial ultra-scurt - 1-2 propoziții + rezultat]" — [Nume Client]

[Număr] oameni au profitat deja. [Număr spots ramase - dacă ai capacity limit real]

## Grab it now:

👉 **[CTA - ex: "Get [X]% Off Now"]** [Link purchase cu discount pre-applied]

⏰ **Expiră: [Dată și oră exactă - ex: "Vineri 23:59 EET"]**

Nu rata asta,
[Numele tău]

P.S. - [Urgență finală - ex: "După [deadline], prețul revine la [preț normal]" sau "Stock limitat la [număr] - FCFS"]

---

**Placeholder-uri pentru AI**:
- `[Nume]`: Prenume (ton: urgent alert)
- `[Timeframe exact]`: Hours/days left (ton: specific countdown, creates urgency)
- `[Nume produs/serviciu]`: Offering (ton: clear)
- `[Discount %]` sau `[pricing special]`: Deal amount (ton: compelling savings)
- `[Motiv real pentru discount]`: Why sale (ton: legitimate reason, not random - builds trust)
- `[Produs/serviciu principal]`: What they get (ton: value reminder)
- `[Feature/bonus #1-2]`: Included items (ton: stack value)
- `[Time-limited bonus]`: Flash sale exclusive (ton: only available NOW)
- `[Preț normal]`: Original price (ton: anchor high)
- `[Preț discount]`: Sale price (ton: emphasize savings)
- `[Amount/Percentage]`: Savings calculation (ton: specific math)
- `[Testimonial ultra-scurt]`: Social proof quick (ton: result-focused)
- `[Nume Client]`: Who said it (ton: real person)
- `[Număr]` oameni cumpărat: Social proof counter (ton: popularity signal)
- `[Număr spots ramase]`: Scarcity (ton: DOAR dacă e real)
- `[CTA]`: Button text (ton: action-oriented)
- `[Link purchase]`: Checkout with discount (ton: pre-applied code, no friction)
- `[Dată și oră exactă]`: Deadline precise (ton: countdown clock)
- `[Numele tău]`: Prenume (ton: urgent messenger)
- `[deadline]` și `[preț normal]`: P.S. reminder (ton: final push)

**Note de personalizare**:
- Scarcity TREBUIE real (time sau quantity limited)
- Reason pentru sale = credibility (milestone, season, clearance)
- Countdown specific = creates urgency ("23:59 Friday" > "this weekend")
- Pre-applied discount link = remove friction
- Social proof = "X people bought" = popularity signal
- Can include: countdown timer visual, stock counter
- FOMO balance: urgent dar nu desperate/spammy
- Guarantee mention = de-risk impulse buy
