---

**Placeholder-uri pentru AI**:
- `[Prenume]`: First name (ton: protective mentor)
- `[Descriere scenario]`: Relatable mistake situation (ton: "you're not alone")
- `[Consecința negativă]`: What happens when mistake occurs (ton: serious but not fear-mongering)
- `[Number]`: How many mistakes (ton: digestible - 3-5)
- `[obiectiv]`: Their goal (ton: what they care about)
- `[Mistake common #1-3]`: Specific mistakes (ton: common, relatable, not shameful)
- `[Explicație scurtă]`: Why it's wrong (ton: educational, not judgmental)
- `[Alternative corectă]`: Right approach (ton: actionable, clear)
- `[Link către resource]`: Free tool/checklist (ton: helpful prevention)
- `[Numele tău]`: Your name (ton: experienced guide)
- `[Specific mistake]`: Biggest mistake example (ton: cautionary tale)
- `[client example]`: Real or anonymized case (ton: credible proof)
- `[cost]`: Quantified impact (ton: tangible consequence)

**Note de personalizare**:
- Mistakes format = engaging, relatable, non-threatening
- Each mistake needs: what it is + why it's wrong + correct alternative
- Use real examples from your experience
- "Quick check" = self-assessment moment (engagement)
- Resource/tool helps them avoid mistakes = practical value
- P.S. teases more valuable content (keeps engagement)
- Can segment by which mistake resonates most (click tracking)
- Position your solution as mistake-prevention system
- Follow with: case study of someone who avoided mistakes, or next educational piece
