**Placeholder-uri pentru AI**:
- `[Nume prospect]`: Prenumele (ton: confident they're qualified)
- `[obiectiv dorit]`: Goal lor (ton: reminder despre de ce sunt aici)
- `[Nume pachet/tier principal]`: Product/service name (ton: branded, clear)
- `[Pricing]`: Cost (ton: transparent, direct - nu "contact us for pricing")
- `[Descriere generală oferire]`: Overview (ton: value-packed, comprehensive)
- `[Component/deliverable 1-3]`: What's included (ton: specific, tangibil - nu "strategic consulting vag")
- `[Bonus/support element]`: Extra value (ton: valuable, relevant)
- `[Garanție]`: Risk reversal (ton: confident, protective - ex: "30 day money-back")
- `[Durată clară]`: Timeframe (ton: realistic, specific)
- `[Nivel de access]`: Support included (ton: clear expectations)
- `[First step]` și următorii pași: Process breakdown (ton: transparent, demystify what happens next)
- `[Timeline]` pentru fiecare pas: When it happens (ton: specific, manageable)
- `[Pricing breakdown]`: Cost structure (ton: transparent, options dacă ai)
- `[Value justification]`: ROI or cost of inaction (ton: business case, rational)
- `[CTA specific]`: Clear next action (ton: simple, low-friction)
- `[Numele tău]`: Prenume (ton: excited to work together)
- `[Urgență sau bonus]`: Scarcity/incentive (ton: DOAR dacă e real - no fake urgency)

**Note de personalizare**:
- Pricing TREBUIE să fie transparent - nu "call for quote"
- Components = specific deliverables, nu buzzwords vagi
- Process breakdown = reduce uncertainty și anxietate
- Timeline realistic = set proper expectations
- Opțiuni de plată dacă oferi = reduce barrier la entry
- Value justification = business case, nu emotional manipulation
- CTA simplu = un pas clar, nu "explore options"
- Garanție sau trial = de-risk decizia
- Evită surprize - totul transparent
- Poate include comparison table dacă ai multiple tiers
