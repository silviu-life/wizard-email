Salut [Nume user],

**Quick favor**: Ai face un scurt video testimonial (2-3 min max)?

4. Tu aprobi, noi publicăm

**Note de personalizare**:
- Video > text for authenticity and emotional impact
- Reach out to users with REAL impressive results
- Make it EASY: phone video OK, simple questions, you handle editing
- Win-win benefits: exposure for them, proof for you
- Full approval before publish = respect their voice
- Alternative (text) option = accommodate camera-shy users
- Can provide: script outline, example videos, recording tips
- Track: conversion rate, video quality, usage effectiveness
- Follow with: edited video for approval, thank you + bonus delivery
