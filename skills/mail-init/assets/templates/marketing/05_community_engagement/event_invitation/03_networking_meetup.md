**Placeholder-uri pentru AI**:
- `[Prenume]`: First name (ton: friendly invite)
- `[Event name]`: Meetup title (ton: memorable branding)
- `[Data + ora]`: When (ton: clear timing)
- `[Location/platform]`: Where (ton: easy to find)
- `[Target audience]`: Who should come (ton: inclusive but relevant)
- `[Preț]`: Cost (ton: low barrier)
- `[Timeframe 1-3]`: Schedule (ton: structured but relaxed)
- `[food/drinks]`: Social element (ton: hospitality)
- `[number]`: Speaker count (ton: content promise)
- `[min]`: Talk length (ton: digestible)
- `[activity]`: Interactive element (ton: engagement)
- `[Names]`: Speakers (ton: credibility or mystery)
- `[users/professionals]`: Audience type (ton: peer group)
- `[Benefit specific]`: Unique value (ton: reason to attend)
- `[Social perk]`: Extra incentive (ton: sweetener)
- `[CTA]`: Action button (ton: easy RSVP)
- `[Link]`: Registration (ton: one-click)
- `[Numele tău]`: Your name (ton: host)
- `[Number]`: RSVP count (ton: social proof)

**Note de personalizare**:
- Networking = community building > content delivery
- Casual vibe = reduce intimidation
- Lightning talks = short, varied content
- Bring a friend = viral growth
- Free/low cost = maximize attendance
- In-person bonuses: food, drinks, swag
- Virtual bonuses: breakout rooms, chat, polls
- Can include: speaker bios, venue details, parking/access info
- Track: RSVP rate, actual attendance, connections made
- Follow with: event recap, photos, next meetup announcement
