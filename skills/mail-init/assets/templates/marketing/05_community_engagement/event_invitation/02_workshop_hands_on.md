# Event Invitation - Hands-On Workshop

---

**Subiect**: [Subiect hands-on: "Build [outcome] live" sau "Hands-on: [skill] workshop" sau "Create [deliverable] în [timeframe]"]

---

Salut [Prenume],

Învățarea pasivă e overrated. Hai să construim ceva **împreună**.

## [Workshop name]: Hands-On [Topic]

**Când**: [Data + ora + timezone] ([Durata - ex: "2 ore"])
**Unde**: [Platform - preferably video-on]
**Cost**: [Preț sau FREE]

## Ce vei construi:

În cele [durata] ore, vei pleca cu:

✅ [Deliverable #1 concret - ex: "Landing page funcțională"]
✅ [Deliverable #2 concret - ex: "Email sequence gata de deploy"]
✅ [Deliverable #3 concret - ex: "Strategy document personalizat"]

**Not theory** - cod/content/strategie reală pe care o poți folosi imediat.

## Cum funcționează:

• **Primele [timeframe]**: Eu demonstrez live
• **Următoarele [timeframe]**: Tu construiești (eu ghidez)
• **Ultimele [timeframe]**: Q&A și troubleshooting

**Vei avea nevoie**: [Prerequisites - ex: "Laptop, cont pe [platform], [software]"]

## Bonuses:

• Recording pentru participants (dacă lipsești parts)
• [Template/starter pack] pentru a accelera
• [Resource library] post-workshop
• Access la [community channel] pentru follow-up questions

**Locuri**: [Limită sau unlimited]

👉 **[CTA - ex: "Salvează-ți locul"]** → [Link registration]

Ne vedem cu laptopurile deschise!

[Numele tău]

P.S. - [Urgency sau social - ex: "Ultimul workshop s-a umplut în [timeframe] - early bird recommended" sau "[Number] already registered"]

**Placeholder-uri pentru AI**:
- `[Prenume]`: First name (ton: active learner)
- `[Workshop name]`: Event title (ton: clear topic)
- `[Topic]`: Subject area (ton: skill focus)
- `[Data + ora]`: When (ton: calendar-ready)
- `[Durata]`: How long (ton: time commitment)
- `[Platform]`: Where (ton: technical requirements)
- `[Preț]`: Cost (ton: transparent)
- `[Deliverable #1-3]`: What they build (ton: tangible outcomes)
- `[timeframe]`: Session segments (ton: structured learning)
- `[Prerequisites]`: What they need (ton: preparation)
- `[Template/starter pack]`: Accelerator resource (ton: head start)
- `[Resource library]`: Post-event value (ton: ongoing support)
- `[community channel]`: Follow-up access (ton: continued learning)
- `[Limită]`: Capacity (ton: scarcity if applicable)
- `[CTA]`: Action button (ton: secure spot)
- `[Link registration]`: Sign-up page (ton: easy registration)
- `[Numele tău]`: Your name (ton: hands-on instructor)
- `[Number]`: Registration count (ton: social proof)

**Note de personalizare**:
- Hands-on = higher perceived value than passive webinar
- Concrete deliverables = clear ROI for time invested
- Prerequisites list = set expectations, prepare attendees
- Bonuses (recording, templates) = reduce risk of missing out
- Limited spots = urgency (if true)
- Social proof (registration count) = momentum
- Can include: agenda breakdown, instructor bio, past participant results
- Track: registration rate, completion rate, deliverable quality
- Follow with: reminder emails, post-workshop survey, next workshop invitation
