- `[Timeframe/tema]`: Period or theme (ton: scope clarity)
- `[Article/resource #1-3]`: Curated content (ton: quality picks)
- `[Autor/Sursa]`: Credit source (ton: attribution)
- `[Time to read]`: Time investment (ton: respect time)
- `[Summary + relevance]`: What + why (ton: value explanation)
- `[Link]`: Direct access (ton: one-click value)

**Note de personalizare**:
- Curation = massive time-saver for audience
- Quality > quantity (3-5 main items)
- Summaries = preview value before click
- Your take = unique value add
- Time estimates = respect reader time
- Source attribution = credibility + ethics
- Can include: tools, articles, videos, podcasts, tweets
- Track: click-through rate by item type
- Follow with: next digest, expanded deep-dive on popular item
