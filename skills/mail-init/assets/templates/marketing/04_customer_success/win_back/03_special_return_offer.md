# Win-back - Special Return Offer (Last Attempt)

---

**Subiect**: [Subiect generous: "Special offer să te aducem înapoi" sau "We miss you - [discount]% off" sau "Last chance: return offer"]

---

Salut [Nume],

E ultimul meu email despre revenire - promit.

**Dar vreau să fac o ultimă ofertă generoas ă:**

## Welcome Back Package:

✅ [Discount]% off pentru [timeframe - ex: "primele 3 luni"]
✅ [Bonus/feature/service inclus gratuit]
✅ [Personal onboarding call] cu mine personal
✅ [Garantie extended - ex: "90 zile money-back"]
✅ [Alt perk special - ex: "Early access la toate new features"]

**Preț**: [Discounted price] (normal: [regular price])

**Valabil până**: [Data specifică]

## De ce ne-am dori înapoi:

[Personal reason - ex: "Feedback-ul tău a îmbunătățit [produs]" sau "Clienții care revin au success rate de [percentage]%"]

**One click**: [CTA - "Claim Return Offer"]

Dacă nu, respect decizia și îți urez mult succes.

[Numele tău]

P.S. - [Graceful exit - ex: "Acesta e ultimul email despre return - după [data], șterg oferta și te las în pace. Promise."]

---

**Placeholder-uri pentru AI**:
- `[Nume]`: Name (ton: final personal appeal)
- `[Discount]%`: Percentage off (ton: significant incentive - 30-50%)
- `[timeframe]`: Discount duration (ton: extended value)
- `[Bonus/feature]`: Extra perk (ton: sweetener)
- `[Personal onboarding]`: VIP treatment (ton: personal investment)
- `[Garantie extended]`: Long safety period (ton: extreme de-risking)
- `[Alt perk]`: Additional benefit (ton: stacking value)
- `[Discounted price]`: Special price (ton: compelling offer)
- `[regular price]`: Normal cost (ton: savings anchor)
- `[Data specifică]`: Deadline (ton: urgency)
- `[Personal reason]`: Why you want them back (ton: genuine appreciation)
- `[percentage]%`: Success stat (ton: they can succeed)
- `[CTA]`: Action button (ton: easy return)
- `[Numele tău]`: Your name (ton: personal appeal)
- `[data]`: When you stop (ton: final deadline)

**Note de personalizare**:
- LAST outreach - respect their decision after
- Most generous offer you can afford
- Stack multiple perks = overwhelming value
- Personal touch = show you care
- Extended guarantee = massive de-risking
- Clear deadline = urgency + closure
- Promise to stop = respect boundaries
- Track: final conversion rate, true LTV of returners
- After this: archive them, move on gracefully
