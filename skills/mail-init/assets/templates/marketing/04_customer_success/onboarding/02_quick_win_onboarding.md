**Pas #1**: [Acțiune specifică simplă]
**Pas #2**: [Acțiune specifică simplă]
**Pas #3**: [Acțiune specifică simplă]

[Link către quick start guide/video - opțional]

## De ce e important:

[Percentage]% care fac asta în primele [timeframe] continuă să folosească [produs] long-term și văd [bigger result].

[Numele tău]

P.S. - [Celebration prompt - ex: "Când reușești, reply cu screenshot - chiar vreau să văd!" sau "Next quick win urmează mâine"]

**Placeholder-uri pentru AI**:
- `[Prenume]`: First name (ton: personal coach)
- `[produs]`: Product name (ton: context)
- `[Result specific]`: First achievement (ton: concrete quick win)
- `[timeframe scurt]`: How long (ton: achievable - 10-30 min)
- `[Acțiune #1-3]`: Simple steps (ton: actionable, clear)
- `[Outcome specific]`: What they'll have (ton: tangible result)
- `[Link guide/video]`: Optional resource (ton: support if needed)
- `[Percentage]`: Success correlation (ton: why it matters)
- `[bigger result]`: Long-term outcome (ton: vision of success)
- `[Link support]`: Help access (ton: safety net)
- `[Numele tău]`: Your name (ton: supportive guide)
- `[Celebration prompt]`: Engagement invitation (ton: share success)

**Note de personalizare**:
- Quick win = first meaningful result fast
- Must be achievable in one sitting (10-30 min)
- Specific steps remove decision fatigue
- Show why it matters (retention correlation)
- Support access = safety if stuck
- Celebration = positive reinforcement
- Works Day 2-3 after initial welcome
- Track: quick win completion rate vs. long-term retention
- Follow with: next quick win, feature deep-dive, or check-in
