- `[Outcome beneficiu]`: Quantified value (ton: ROI demonstration)
- `[Plan name]`: Subscription tier (ton: clear)
- `[Pricing pentru renewal]`: Renewal cost (ton: transparent - no surprises)
- `[Feature/benefit continuat #1-2]`: What continues (ton: ongoing value)
- `[New feature/improvement]`: Recent addition (ton: "you're getting more value")
- `[Support level]`: Access level (ton: reassurance)
- `[Update semnificativ]`: Product improvement (ton: shows investment, momentum)
- `[dată]`: Renewal date repeat (ton: clear timeline)
- `[plan superior]`, `[plan inferior]`: Other options (ton: flexibility)
- `[Link manage subscription]`: Self-service control (ton: easy management)
- `[Numele tău]`: Prenume (ton: personal touch)
- `[Titlul]`: Role (ton: here to help)
- `[Appreciation]` sau `[support offer]`: P.S. care (ton: relationship nurture)

**Note de personalizare**:
- Timing: 14-30 days notice = respectful, no surprise charges
- Value recap = remind them WHY they subscribed
- Usage metrics personalized = "look what you achieved with us"
- New features = show product evolution, ongoing investment
- Transparent pricing = no hidden fees
- Easy management = flexibility builds trust (upgrade/downgrade/pause)
- Proactive payment update reminder = reduce involuntary churn
- Tone = appreciation, not "your card will be charged"
- Offer help = CS support available
- Can include: usage dashboard link, annual plan discount offer
