# Renewal - Upgrade Opportunity at Renewal

---

**Subiect**: [Subiect growth: "Time to level up?" sau "Renewal + upgrade opportunity" sau "Your growth path cu [produs]"]

---

Salut [Prenume],

Renewal-ul vine în [timeframe] - perfect moment să evaluezi unde ești și unde vrei să ajungi.

## Unde ești acum:

[Plan actual] te-a dus la:
• [Achievement #1]
• [Achievement #2]
• [Current limitation they might be feeling]

## Unde ai putea fi:

Cu [Higher tier/plan], clienții similari au:
• [Next level result #1 cu metrică]
• [Next level result #2 cu metrică]
• [Next level result #3 cu metrică]

**Diferența**: [Key features unlocked în upgrade]

## Renewal options:

**Option 1**: Renew [plan actual] la [preț]
**Option 2**: Upgrade la [higher plan] la [preț diferență] (+[features/value])

**Upgrade bonus**: Dacă upgrade acum, includ [bonus/discount/perk].

Ce simți că e potrivit pentru [obiectivul lor]?

[CTA - ex: "Review Upgrade Options" sau "Schedule Growth Call"]

[Numele tău]

P.S. - [Social proof - ex: "[Percentage]% care upgrade la renewal văd [specific improved result]"]

---

**Placeholder-uri pentru AI**:
- `[Prenume]`: First name (ton: growth partner)
- `[timeframe]`: Renewal timing (ton: opportunity window)
- `[Plan actual]`: Current tier (ton: acknowledge progress)
- `[Achievement #1-2]`: What they've accomplished (ton: celebrate wins)
- `[Current limitation]`: Pain point at current tier (ton: growth constraint)
- `[Higher tier/plan]`: Upgrade option (ton: next level)
- `[Next level result #1-3]`: What upgrade enables (ton: aspirational outcomes)
- `[Key features]`: What changes (ton: capability expansion)
- `[plan actual]`: Current plan name (ton: continuity option)
- `[preț]`: Renewal cost (ton: baseline)
- `[higher plan]`: Upgrade tier (ton: growth option)
- `[preț diferență]`: Additional investment (ton: transparent cost)
- `[features/value]`: What extra includes (ton: value justification)
- `[bonus/discount/perk]`: Renewal upgrade incentive (ton: sweetener)
- `[obiectivul lor]`: Their goals (ton: personalized framing)
- `[CTA]`: Action (ton: explore options)
- `[Numele tău]`: Your name (ton: growth advisor)
- `[Percentage]`: Upgrade success rate (ton: social proof)
- `[specific improved result]`: Upgrade outcome (ton: proof of value)

**Note de personalizare**:
- Renewal = natural evaluation moment
- Frame as "growth opportunity" not just renewal
- Show what they've achieved at current level
- Contrast with what's possible at next level
- Transparent options: stay same OR upgrade
- Upgrade incentive at renewal = conversion booster
- Personalize based on their usage and goals
- Track: upgrade rate at renewal vs. mid-contract
- Can include: tier comparison chart, ROI calculator
- Follow with: upgrade confirmation or standard renewal
