# Upsell/Cross-sell - Complementary Product

---

**Subiect**: [Subiect complement: "Perfect cu [produs actual]" sau "Missing piece: [complement]" sau "Upgrade your [result]"]

---

Salut [Prenume],

Văd că [produs principal] merge bine pentru tine ([usage metric sau result observed]).

**Quick thought**: Mulți clienți care folosesc [produs principal] adaugă și [produs complementar] pentru [specific enhanced outcome].

## Cum funcționează împreună:

[Produs principal] → [Rezultat actual]

[Produs principal] **+** [Produs complementar] → [Enhanced result - de ce e mai bun]

## De exemplu:

[Client example] folosește ambele și [specific result with metric].

**Ce primești cu [produs complementar]**:
✅ [Beneficiu #1]
✅ [Beneficiu #2]
✅ [Beneficiu #3]

**Bundle offer**: [Produs complementar] la [discount] când îl adaugi la [produs actual].

[CTA - ex: "Add to My Plan"]

[Numele tău]

P.S. - [Alternative - ex: "Sau poți încerca gratis [trial period] să vezi dacă îți convine"]

---

**Placeholder-uri pentru AI**:
- `[Prenume]`: First name (ton: continuing success)
- `[produs principal]`: Current product (ton: acknowledge what they have)
- `[usage metric]`: How they're using it (ton: observed success)
- `[result observed]`: Their achievement (ton: celebrate wins)
- `[produs complementar]`: Add-on product (ton: natural extension)
- `[specific enhanced outcome]`: Better result (ton: improved performance)
- `[Rezultat actual]`: Current outcome (ton: baseline)
- `[Enhanced result]`: With complement (ton: upgraded outcome)
- `[Client example]`: Social proof (ton: relatable user)
- `[specific result with metric]`: Their success (ton: concrete proof)
- `[Beneficiu #1-3]`: Complement benefits (ton: added value)
- `[discount]`: Bundle pricing (ton: incentive to add)
- `[CTA]`: Action (ton: easy addition)
- `[Numele tău]`: Your name (ton: advisor)
- `[trial period]`: Risk-free test (ton: try before commit)

**Note de personalizare**:
- Complement = naturally enhances current product
- Must have genuine synergy (not forced upsell)
- Show how they work TOGETHER (1+1=3 effect)
- Social proof from similar customers
- Bundle discount = incentive to add now
- Trial option = remove purchase anxiety
- Works best after successful usage of main product
- Can include: comparison chart, demo video, case study
- Track: which product combos have highest LTV
- Follow with: onboarding for new product, usage tips
