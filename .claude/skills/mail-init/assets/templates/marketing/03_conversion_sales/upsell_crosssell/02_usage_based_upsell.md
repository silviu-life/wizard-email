# Upsell/Cross-sell - Usage-Based Upgrade

---

**Subiect**: [Subiect data: "You're maxing out [feature]" sau "Time to upgrade [produs]?" sau "Usage alert: [metric]"]

---

Salut [Prenume],

Am observat că [usage pattern specific - ex: "folosești [feature] la 95% capacity" sau "ai atins [limit] de [metric]"].

**Asta e awesome** - înseamnă că [produs actual] funcționează pentru tine!

## Dar probabil simți limitările:

• [Limitation #1 based on usage]
• [Limitation #2 based on usage]
• [Limitation #3 based on usage]

## Cu [Upgrade/Premium tier]:

✅ [Benefit #1] - [specific outcome]
✅ [Benefit #2] - [specific outcome]
✅ [Benefit #3] - [specific outcome]
✅ [Benefit #4] - **[Most compelling for their usage pattern]**

**Cost difference**: +[price increase] ([compare to value gained])

**ROI**: Clienții care upgrade văd [ROI metric] în [timeframe].

Interested? [CTA - ex: "Upgrade Now" sau "See Full Comparison"]

[Numele tău]

P.S. - [Risk reversal - ex: "Poți downgrade oricând" sau "Primul [timeframe] include și [bonus/trial period]"]

---

**Placeholder-uri pentru AI**:
- `[Prenume]`: First name (ton: data-informed advisor)
- `[usage pattern]`: Specific behavior (ton: you're watching, you care)
- `[feature]`: What they're maxing (ton: specific constraint)
- `[limit]`: Usage ceiling (ton: concrete threshold)
- `[metric]`: Measurable usage (ton: quantified)
- `[produs actual]`: Current plan (ton: acknowledge success)
- `[Limitation #1-3]`: Current constraints (ton: pain points they feel)
- `[Upgrade/Premium tier]`: Next plan name (ton: clear option)
- `[Benefit #1-4]`: Upgrade features (ton: solve limitations)
- `[specific outcome]`: What they enable (ton: results-focused)
- `[Most compelling]`: Key benefit for their pattern (ton: personalized value)
- `[price increase]`: Additional cost (ton: transparent)
- `[compare to value]`: ROI framing (ton: investment perspective)
- `[ROI metric]`: Customer data (ton: proof of value)
- `[timeframe]`: How fast (ton: realistic timeline)
- `[CTA]`: Action (ton: clear next step)
- `[Numele tău]`: Your name (ton: growth partner)
- `[bonus/trial period]`: Risk reducer (ton: try before commit)

**Note de personalizare**:
- Usage data = perfect timing for upsell
- Must have actual usage tracking to trigger this
- Show THEIR specific limitations (not generic)
- Benefits directly solve their usage pain points
- Transparent pricing = build trust
- ROI data from similar customers = social proof
- Risk reversal (downgrade option) = removes anxiety
- Works best when they're actively hitting limits
- Can include: usage dashboard, comparison chart
- Follow with: upgrade confirmation or check-in later
- Track: conversion rate by usage threshold
