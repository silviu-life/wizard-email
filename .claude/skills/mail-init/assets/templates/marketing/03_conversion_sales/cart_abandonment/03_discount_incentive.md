# Cart Abandonment - Discount Incentive (Final Push)

---

**Subiect**: [Subiect offer: "[Discount]% off [produs] în coș" sau "Special offer pentru tine" sau "Here's [savings] pentru a decide"]

---

Salut [Prenume],

Văd că încă te gândești la [produs].

**Vreau să te ajut să decizi** - așa că îți ofer [discount]% off doar pentru tine.

## Your special price:

~~[Preț original]~~ → **[Preț cu discount]**

**Economisești [savings amount]**

**Cod**: [UNIQUE_CODE] (expiră în [timeframe - ex: "48 ore"])

[CTA button - "Claim My Discount"]

Include:
✅ [Feature/beneficiu #1]
✅ [Feature/beneficiu #2]
✅ [Garantie]

**Nota**: Codul funcționează doar pentru [produs] din coșul tău, doar pentru [timeframe].

[Numele tău]

P.S. - [Final urgency - ex: "Dacă nu e pentru tine, no worries - șterg codul după [deadline]" sau "[Number] alți clienți au folosit acest discount și [rezultat]"]

---

**Placeholder-uri pentru AI**:
- `[Prenume]`: First name (ton: personal offer)
- `[produs]`: Abandoned product (ton: specific item)
- `[discount]`: Percentage off (ton: quantified value - typically 10-20%)
- `[Preț original]`: Regular price (ton: anchor)
- `[Preț cu discount]`: Discounted price (ton: special deal)
- `[savings amount]`: Money saved (ton: dollar value)
- `[UNIQUE_CODE]`: Personal discount code (ton: exclusive to them)
- `[timeframe]`: Expiration (ton: 24-48h urgency)
- `[CTA button]`: Action text (ton: claim value)
- `[Feature/beneficiu #1-2]`: Product highlights (ton: reminder of value)
- `[Garantie]`: Risk reversal (ton: safety)
- `[Numele tău]`: Your name (ton: personal generosity)
- `[deadline]`: Code expiration (ton: final deadline)
- `[Number]` clienți: Social proof (ton: others took action)
- `[rezultat]`: Customer outcome (ton: what they achieved)

**Note de personalizare**:
- Last-ditch effort with discount
- Unique personal code = feels exclusive
- Short expiration (24-48h) = strong urgency
- Don't over-discount (10-20% typ ical)
- Clear value reminder before discount
- Guarantee reduces risk
- "If not for you, no worries" = pressure relief
- Track: discount recovery rate vs. margin loss
- If they don't convert, let them go
- Can A/B test different discount levels
- Works best 72h-5 days after abandonment
- Follow with: nothing (they're gone) or re-engagement much later
