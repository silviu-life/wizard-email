# Limited Offer - Expiring Discount/Coupon

---

**Subiect**: [Subiect urgent: "[Discount]% off expiră [timeframe]" sau "Last chance: [offer name]" sau "[Hours/Days] left pentru [discount]"]

---

Salut [Prenume],

Quick heads up: [Discount/offer name] expiră în **[timeframe exact]**.

## Ce pierzi după [deadline]:

❌ [Discount] discount ([savings amount])
❌ [Bonus/extra dacă există]
❌ [Alt beneficiu time-sensitive]

## Ce câștigi dacă acționezi acum:

✅ [Produs/serviciu] la [preț cu discount]
✅ [Bonus inclus]
✅ [Garanție/extra]

**[Timeframe] rămas** → [CTA button cu cod discount]

**Cod**: [DISCOUNT_CODE] (aplică automat la link)

[Numele tău]

P.S. - [Urgency note - ex: "După [deadline], prețul revine la [normal price]" sau "[Number] oameni au folosit codul azi"]

---

**Placeholder-uri pentru AI**:
- `[Prenume]`: First name (ton: helpful friend reminding)
- `[Discount/offer name]`: Offer description (ton: clear what's expiring)
- `[timeframe exact]`: Specific time remaining (ton: precise urgency - "18 ore", "2 zile")
- `[deadline]`: When it ends (ton: concrete cutoff)
- `[Discount]`: Percentage/amount off (ton: quantified savings)
- `[savings amount]`: Money saved (ton: dollar value)
- `[Bonus/extra]`: Additional perks expiring (ton: compounding loss)
- `[Alt beneficiu]`: Other time-sensitive value (ton: stacking urgency)
- `[Produs/serviciu]`: What they get (ton: clear offering)
- `[preț cu discount]`: Discounted price (ton: final cost)
- `[Bonus inclus]`: What's included (ton: value add)
- `[Garanție/extra]`: Risk reversal (ton: safety)
- `[CTA button]`: Action text (ton: immediate action)
- `[DISCOUNT_CODE]`: Coupon code (ton: easy redemption)
- `[Numele tău]`: Your name (ton: reminder friend)
- `[normal price]`: Regular price (ton: contrast anchor)
- `[Number]` oameni: Social proof (ton: others acting)

**Note de personalizare**:
- Simple, direct, time-focused
- Use exact time remaining (not vague "soon")
- Show what they LOSE (loss aversion psychology)
- Show what they GAIN (positive framing)
- Auto-apply code at link = remove friction
- Works well 24-48h before expiration
- Can send series: 3 days, 24h, 6h, 1h warnings
- Track: conversion spike timing for future campaigns
- Countdown timer in email = powerful visual urgency
- P.S. adds social proof or price increase reminder
- Follow with: thank you email for purchasers, final final chance for non-buyers
