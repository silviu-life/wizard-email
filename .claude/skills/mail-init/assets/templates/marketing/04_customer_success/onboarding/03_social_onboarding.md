[Platform #1 - ex: "Slack/Discord"]: [Link] - Daily discussions, quick help

**Note de personalizare**:
- Community = accelerated learning + reduced churn
- Multiple platforms = meet them where they are
- Trending topics = show active engagement
- Specific intro location = reduce intimidation
- Success correlation = why it matters
- Works Day 5-7 after onboarding basics
- Can include: intro template, community guidelines
- Track: community engagement vs. product retention
- Follow with: community highlights, member spotlights
