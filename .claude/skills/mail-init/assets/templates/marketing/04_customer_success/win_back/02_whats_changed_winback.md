# Win-back - "What's Changed" Approach

---

**Subiect**: [Subiect evolution: "[Produs] e diferit acum" sau "What's changed since you left" sau "We've evolved - second look?"]

---

Salut [Nume fost client],

De când ai plecat acum [timeframe], [produs] a evoluat destul de mult.

## Ce s-a schimbat:

✨ **[Major improvement #1]** - [Common pain point it solves]
✨ **[Major improvement #2]** - [Common pain point it solves]
✨ **[Major improvement #3]** - [Common pain point it solves]

**Most significant**: [Biggest change relevant to why they likely churned]

## Proof:

• [Metric showing improvement - ex: "Speed 3x faster"]
• [Customer satisfaction data - ex: "NPS crescut de la X la Y"]
• [Adoption stat - ex: "[Number]% dintre churned customers au revenit"]

**Dacă motivul pentru care ai plecat a fost [likely churn reason], l-am addressed.**

## No-risk return:

• [Trial period] free pentru a testa schimbările
• [Garantie extended]
• [Personal onboarding/setup call]

Worth a second look? [CTA]

[Numele tău]

P.S. - [Humble note - ex: "Chiar dacă nu revii, feedback-ul despre ce ne lipsește încă ar ajuta enorm"]

---

**Placeholder-uri pentru AI**:
- `[Nume fost client]`: Full/first name (ton: respectful reconnection)
- `[timeframe]`: Since churn (ton: acknowledge time passed)
- `[produs]`: Product name (ton: evolved version)
- `[Major improvement #1-3]`: Key changes (ton: substantial evolution)
- `[Common pain point]`: What it fixes (ton: address their problems)
- `[Biggest change]`: Most relevant improvement (ton: personalized to churn reason)
- `[Metric showing improvement]`: Quantified progress (ton: concrete proof)
- `[Customer satisfaction]`: Validation data (ton: others confirm improvement)
- `[Number]%` returners: Win-back success rate (ton: social proof)
- `[likely churn reason]`: Why they left (ton: direct acknowledgment)
- `[Trial period]`: Risk-free test (ton: try improvements)
- `[Garantie extended]`: Safety net (ton: confidence in changes)
- `[Personal onboarding]`: VIP treatment (ton: we value return)
- `[CTA]`: Action button (ton: low-pressure invitation)
- `[Numele tău]`: Your name (ton: personal accountability)

**Note de personalizare**:
- Evolution narrative = "we learned from you leaving"
- Address likely churn reasons proactively
- Quantify improvements with metrics
- Returnee success rate = powerful social proof
- Generous win-back offer = lower barrier
- Feedback request even if no return = gracious
- Segment by known churn reason if available
- Track: win-back conversion rate, second-churn rate
- Follow with: onboarding if return, thank you if feedback only
