**Placeholder-uri pentru AI**:
- `[Prenume]`: First name (ton: consultant peer)
- `[context despre situația lor]`: What you know about them (ton: researched, relevant)
- `[tool/process/challenge]`: Their current situation (ton: understanding)
- `[problem]`: Pain point they're trying to solve (ton: empathetic)
- `[tip audit]`: Type of free review (ton: specific, valuable)
- `[current setup/process]`: What they'll show (ton: collaborative)
- `[Finding/insight #1-3]`: Typical audit findings (ton: preview of value)
- `[produs]`: Your product (ton: solution tailored to their context)
- `[Deliverable concret]`: What they get regardless (ton: generous, risk-free)
- `[Link calendar]`: Scheduling link (ton: easy action)
- `[Numele tău]`: Your name (ton: expert consultant)
- `[Titlul]`: Your role (ton: credibility)
- `[număr]`: How many audits you've done (ton: experience proof)
- `[tip companii]`: Client type (ton: relevant social proof)
- `[insight comun valuable]`: Typical finding (ton: credible preview)

**Note de personalizare**:
- Free audit = massive value, builds trust instantly
- Must deliver REAL value in audit, not just sales pitch
- Works best for complex B2B solutions
- 30 min timeframe = respects time while delivering value
- Deliverable (PDF/checklist) = takeaway value even if no sale
- Demo becomes natural extension of audit findings
- High conversion rate because you're solving real problem live
- Segment: those who book = highly qualified leads
- Can record session (with permission) for case study later
- Follow-up: Share recording + deliverable + proposal
