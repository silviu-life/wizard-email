# Warm Introduction - LinkedIn Connection Leverage

---

**Subiect**: [Subiect LinkedIn-context: "Loved your post about [topic]" sau "Quick question after seeing [content]" sau "Following up on [interaction]"]

---

Salut [Prenume],

Am văzut [content-ul/postarea/comentariul tău] despre [topic specific] pe LinkedIn și mi-a rezonat instant.

**Ce m-a lovit**: [Insight specific din content-ul lor - cu propriile tale cuvinte, 1-2 propoziții]

Lucrez cu [tipul de companii/profesioniști] care se confruntă cu [problema relevantă pentru domeniul lor], și recent am [realizare/rezultat specific relevant].

## Quick question

[Întrebare inteligentă legată de content-ul lor - arată că ai înțeles problema lor mai profund - ex: "Cum navighezi [challenge specific]?" sau "Care e experiența ta cu [aspect relevant]?"]

Dacă e relevant, aș putea împărtăși [valoare specifică - insight/resource/case study].

Fără presiuni - doar respect pentru expertise-ul tău în [domeniul lor].

Cheers,
[Numele tău]
[Titlul] | [Compania - opțional]

P.S. - [Connection reinforcement - ex: "Văd că urmărești și pe [influencer comun] - content-ul lui despre [topic] e gold" sau "LinkedIn-ul tău e o resursă genială pentru [industrie]"]

---

**Placeholder-uri pentru AI**:
- `[Prenume]`: First name only (ton: professional-casual)
- `[content-ul/postarea/comentariul tău]`: Specific LinkedIn activity (ton: show you actually engaged)
- `[topic specific]`: Their content topic (ton: demonstrate understanding)
- `[Insight specific]`: Your takeaway from their content (ton: thoughtful, not generic praise)
- `[tipul de companii/profesioniști]`: Your ICP (ton: relevant to their world)
- `[problema relevantă]`: Pain point in their domain (ton: shows you understand their challenges)
- `[realizare/rezultat specific]`: Your relevant achievement (ton: credibility without bragging)
- `[Întrebare inteligentă]`: Question about their challenge (ton: curious, expert-level understanding)
- `[challenge specific]`: Specific problem in their domain (ton: shows deep understanding)
- `[valoare specifică]`: What you can share (ton: generous, relevant)
- `[domeniul lor]`: Their area of expertise (ton: respect for their knowledge)
- `[Numele tău]`: Your first name (ton: approachable)
- `[Titlul]`: Your role (ton: establishes credibility)
- `[Compania]`: Optional company name (ton: additional credibility)
- `[Connection reinforcement]`: P.S. to strengthen connection (ton: genuine shared interest)

**Note de personalizare**:
- LinkedIn warm intro = already have context from their profile/content
- Reference specific content they shared (post, comment, article)
- Show you understand their POV before pitching anything
- Question should be intelligent enough to demonstrate expertise
- Offer value FIRST (insight, resource, case study)
- P.S. can reference mutual connections, shared interests, or their content quality
- Goal: Start conversation, not close sale
- Works best within 48h of LinkedIn interaction
- Can mention if you have mutual connections or shared groups
- Keep it conversational - LinkedIn is less formal than cold email
