**Placeholder-uri pentru AI**:
- `[Nume subscriber]`: Prenume (ton: sharing discoveries cu prieten)
- `[Număr]`: Count insights (ton: specific, 3-5 ideal)
- `[Titlu resource/insight #1-3]`: Headline pentru fiecare (ton: intriguing, clear value)
- `[Descriere]`: What it is (ton: curator explaining, not selling)
- `[Link către resource]`: URL external (ton: generous sharing, credite sursa)
- `[Why it matters]`: Practical application (ton: connect to their world)
- `[Observație personală]`: Your unique take (ton: thoughtful, perspective-driven, not obvious)
- `[Action Item]`: Today's task (ton: simple, specific, 10-15 min max)
- `[Numele tău]`: Prenume (ton: generous expert)
- `[Întrebare engagement]` sau `[behind-the-scenes]`: P.S. hook (ton: involve them, peek behind curtain)

**Note de personalizare**:
- ZERO pitch about your product/service - pure generosity
- Resources pot fi: tools, articles, books, podcasts, threads, research
- Curation > creation - aggregate best stuff
- "Why it matters" = bridge la practical application
- Quick thought = your unique perspective, editorial voice
- Action item = one thing they can do TODAY
- Link-uri = external, credit sources
- Poate include: "found this", "stumbled upon", "been thinking about"
- Tone = trusted curator sharing discoveries
- P.S. engagement = build conversation, not monologue
