**Placeholder-uri pentru AI**:
- `[Nume subscriber]`: Prenume (ton: sharing inspiration)
- `[Nume companie client]`: Client name (ton: real, cu permisiune share)
- `[Industria]`: Sector (ton: relatable cu subscriber)
- `[Mărime companie]`: Size metrics (ton: peer-level, achievable)
- `[Nume persoană + rol]`: Client contact (ton: real person, credibility)
- `[timeframe]`: Cât timp în urmă (ton: recent, relevant)
- `[problema principală detaliată]`: Core challenge (ton: mirror subscriber's potential pain)
- `[Symptom/pain point #1-3]`: Manifestări concrete (ton: specific, relatable, measurable)
- `[Impact]`: Business consequence (ton: serious, quantified)
- `[Quote despre frustrare]`: Emotional state (ton: vulnerable, authentic)
- `[abordare/metodă]`: Solution overview (ton: clear process)
- `[Pas implementare #1-3]`: What was done (ton: replicable, clear)
- `[Durată implementare]`: How long (ton: realistic timeline)
- `[perioada]`: Results timeframe (ton: achievable, not "overnight")
- `[Metrică #1-3]`: Before/after numbers (ton: impressive but credible)
- `[% improvement]`: Percentage change (ton: specific calculation)
- `[Calculul ROI]`: Financial impact (ton: transparent, business case)
- `[Testimonial detaliat]`: Full quote (ton: natural language, journey + emotions + results)
- `[Takeaway #1-3]`: Learnings pentru subscriber (ton: actionable, not just "inspiring")
- `[Numele tău]`: Prenume (ton: storyteller)
- `[CTA soft]`: Next step (ton: invitation, not pushy)

**Note de personalizare**:
- Client TREBUIE similar cu subscriber ICP (industrie, size, challenges)
- Metrics = specific numbers, nu "dramatic improvement"
- Timeline realistic = builds trust ("60 zile" > "2 săptămâni miracol")
- Testimonial = limbaj real, imperfect, uman (nu corporate speak)
- Include struggle și doubt = makes transformation credibil
- Takeaways = actionable learnings, nu doar "inspirație vagă"
- Visual proof: screenshots dashboards, before/after charts, video testimonial
- Link către full case study cu mai multe detalii
