**Placeholder-uri pentru AI**:
- `[Nume fost client]`: Name (ton: respectful goodbye)
- `[produs/subscription]`: What they canceled (ton: context)
- `[Number]`: Question count (ton: brief respect for time)
- `[Feature X]`: Commonly requested (ton: known gap)
- `[care?]`: Which competitor (ton: market intelligence)
- `[Text box]`: Open feedback (ton: unconstrained honesty)
- `[Link survey]`: Short form (ton: easy completion)
- `[Offer]`: Last retention attempt (ton: genuine willingness to fix)
- `[Link sau reply]`: Contact method (ton: accessible)
- `[Numele tău]`: Your name (ton: personal accountability)

**Note de personalizare**:
- Exit survey = critical product intelligence
- Multiple choice + open-ended = quantitative + qualitative
- No hard feelings tone = encourage honesty
- Last retention offer = some will reconsider
- Competitor question = market intelligence
- Keep VERY short (3-5 questions) = maximize completion
- Timing: immediately after cancel = fresh memory
- Can offer: incentive for completion, discount to return
- Track: churn reason patterns, successful saves
- Follow with: product improvements, win-back campaign later
