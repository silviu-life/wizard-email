# Survey - Product Direction Input

---

**Subiect**: [Subiect strategic: "Shape [produs] future" sau "Strategic input needed" sau "Your vision pentru [produs]"]

---

Salut [Prenume],

Planific [timeframe - ex: "Q[trimestru]" sau "2025"] pentru [produs] și vreau să știu: **unde vrei să mergem?**

## [Number] strategic questions ([timeframe - ex: "5 min"]):

**1. Biggest frustration** cu [produs] acum?

**2. If you could add ONE feature**, care ar fi?

**3. What would make [produs] 10x mai valoros** pentru tine?

**4. Ce te face să renunți** la [produs]? (Honest answer wanted)

**5. În [timeframe - ex: "12 luni"], cum arată success** cu [produs]?

[Link survey - simple form, nu complex]

## Why your input matters:

• Influențezi roadmap direct
• Vei vedea rezultatele aggregate
• Early access la features votate de comunitate

**Promise**: Share results cu toți respondents + updated roadmap.

Votul tău = viitorul produsului.

[Numele tău]

P.S. - [Incentive - ex: "Toți respondents primesc [bonus/credit/early access]"]

---

**Placeholder-uri pentru AI**:
- `[Prenume]`: First name (ton: valued strategist)
- `[timeframe]`: Planning period (ton: forward-looking)
- `[produs]`: Product name (ton: shared ownership)
- `[Number]`: Question count (ton: manageable scope)
- `[Link survey]`: Simple form (ton: easy participation)
- `[Numele tău]`: Your name (ton: accountable leader)
- `[bonus/credit]`: Participation reward (ton: appreciation)

**Note de personalizare**:
- Strategic questions > feature requests
- Open-ended = uncover unexpected insights
- "What would make you quit" = honest churn prevention
- Success vision = understand their goals
- Promise to share results = accountability
- Early access to voted features = reward participation
- Keep survey short (5-7 questions max)
- Can include: industry benchmarks, competitor comparison
- Track: completion rate, theme patterns, feature votes
- Follow with: results summary, roadmap reveal, thank you
